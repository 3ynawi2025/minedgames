### الوصف (إنجليزي)

Brain Games is your pocket collection of relaxing logic puzzles — Number Match and Sudoku, with more games on the way.

◆ Number Match: find pairs of equal numbers or pairs that add up to 10, and clear the board. Train your brain with the classic paper-and-pen number game, now on your phone.

◆ Sudoku: fill the 9×9 grid so every row, column and 3×3 box contains 1–9. Four difficulties, pencil notes, smart hints and a daily challenge.

Why you will love it:
• 100% free — no ads, no paywalls. Just pure puzzle fun.
• 100% offline — play anywhere, no wifi needed.
• Fair and transparent — no hidden algorithm tricks.
• Dark mode, 5 beautiful themes, and colorblind-friendly.
• Daily challenges, streaks and trophies that keep you coming back.

Challenge your brain — one game at a time.

Brain Games — مجموعتك من ألغاز العقل المريحة: Number Match وسودوكو، مع ألعاب قادمة.

◆ سودوكو: املأ شبكة 9×9 بحيث يحتوي كل صف وعمود ومربع 3×3 على الأرقام 1–9. أربع صعوبات، ملاحظات بالقلم، تلميحات ذكية، وتحدٍّ يومي.
