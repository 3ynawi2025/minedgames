This app, "Brain Match: Number Puzzles", is an entirely original product built completely from scratch by a small independent team. There is no template, no third-party source code, and no skeleton of another app behind it. Every screen, mechanic, illustration, icon and animation was designed and coded by hand specifically for this release.

Version 1.2 is not a generic number-puzzle clone. It is one cohesive original product defined by a travel and collection identity that does not exist anywhere in this category.

1. World-Tour narrative progression. The game is structured as a journey through 20 real cities - Dubai, Paris, Cairo, Tokyo and more. Each city has 10 rounds, custom landmark illustrations and its own country flag, and unlocking the next city requires completing the previous one.

2. Postcard Collection book. Finishing a city earns a collectible 3D claymorphism postcard featuring the landmark, the country flag, a fun cultural fact and the date it was earned. This collection and education meta-game is unique among number-puzzle apps.

3. A branded signature mechanic called "Linked Cities". Players match pairs of numbers across empty gaps in a row, column or diagonal, joined by animated gradient connection threads. This is our own designed mechanic, not copied from any other title.

4. A custom claymorphism design system with a bespoke thin-stroke icon set and the Baloo rounded typeface. Classic puzzle formats are re-imagined, not duplicated: 2048 becomes "Skyline", where tiles evolve into glowing city blocks, and Sudoku becomes "City Planner", themed with a paper-grain and skyline backdrop. Each city unlock plays a choreographed win sequence.

5. Full bilingual Arabic (RTL) and English support, which is rare in this genre.

6. 100% offline play with no forced ads. Progress is rewarded through a coin economy driven by win and rank rewards, tiered hints, a daily challenge and eight ranks. In-app purchases offer only optional coin packs.

Thank you for reconsidering. This is not a repackaged clone; it is one original, hand-built product with a unique travel and collection identity.
